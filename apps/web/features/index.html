Product capabilities

Live context now. A careful record later.

SessionSignal combines local, consent-based camera analysis with an authenticated facilitator dashboard and durable session reports.

LIVE

Session signal timeline

Rolling observable estimates with a session average and clear unavailable or stale states.

SIGNALS

Gaze, eyes, pose, and drowsiness

Local heuristics are summarized every five seconds instead of streaming frames.

ACT

Review prompts

Transparent threshold-based suggestions help facilitators decide when a check-in or break may help.

REVIEW

Historical analysis

Heatmaps, participant comparisons, detailed charts, printable reports, and CSV exports remain available.

Honest about what the signals mean.

SessionSignal outputs are camera-based estimates. Gaze is not proof of attention, a rule-based state label is not a fact about a person's feelings, and missing signal is not disengagement.

The product supports human review in classes, workshops, training, coaching, and other live sessions. It must not automate grades, discipline, attendance penalties, or other high-stakes decisions.
